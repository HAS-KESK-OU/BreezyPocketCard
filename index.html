Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

HAS-KESK OU publishes Breezy Pocket Card for iPhone and iPad and is responsible for the information described here. You can play the built-in puzzles without creating an account. Your progress stays on your device. The app also connects to online services that process installation, usage and diagnostic information.

What the game saves on your device

The game saves your campaign progress, unfinished puzzles and expeditions, daily challenge results, achievements, and the animal designs you unlock and equip. It also remembers your tutorial progress, sound and music levels, haptics, and display and play preferences. The built-in puzzles use this local save to resume play. The game does not offer account-based cloud sync for these records.

What goes online and why

  • Startup and service access. The app creates a random identifier for the installation and sends it with the game identifier when it requests configuration. It may also send a result code for a startup access check. We use these records to deliver the appropriate configuration, determine service availability and diagnose problems. Servers handling a connection also receive its IP address and technical details such as the time of the request.
  • Installation and usage measurement. We use Adjust GmbH to understand installations, sessions and activity from our own app and links. When enabled, Adjust can process an Adjust identifier, Apple's identifier for vendors (IDFV), installation source, device and operating-system information, app or SDK version, and event timestamps. These records can be associated with the same installation or device. Our iOS integration disables reading Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • Services opened inside the app. Online pages may use cookies, local storage or session tokens to remember a session or preferences. If an online feature reports an event to Adjust, the event may include its name and details. For example, a registration event can record completion, and a balance top-up event can include the amount and currency. These events depend on which online features are enabled and used.
  • Support and privacy requests. If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any details or attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer you, investigate the issue and carry out your request.

We use measurement data for our own analytics. We do not send device data to advertising platforms or link it with information from other companies' apps or websites for advertising or advertising measurement.

Who receives information

Our hosting and configuration providers process technical requests, our email provider handles messages you send us, and Adjust processes measurement data on our behalf. We require providers handling this information to use it only for the agreed purposes and to provide at least the protection described in this policy. You can read more in Adjust's Privacy Policy.

We may disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect users, investigate abuse or defend legal claims. Apple handles App Store and TestFlight distribution under Apple's Privacy Policy.

How long we keep information and how to delete it

On your device. To remove local progress and settings, use Delete App on your iPhone or iPad. Offloading the app preserves its documents and data. A device backup may retain a copy according to your backup settings.

On our systems. We keep configuration and diagnostic records for as long as needed to operate the service and investigate access issues. We retain identifiable measurement records for as long as needed to analyze usage, and support messages for as long as needed to resolve and follow up on a request. We then delete or anonymize the records unless a legal or security obligation requires us to retain them longer.

Deleting the app removes its local data. To request deletion of records already sent to us or our providers, email us. We will review the request, work with our providers where needed, and explain any limits on deletion.

Your privacy choices

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, restrict or object to its use, or complain to your local data protection authority. If processing relies on your consent, you can withdraw it by contacting us. Withdrawal applies to future processing.

Where a legal basis is required, we process information to deliver services you request, pursue our legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving the game, comply with legal obligations, or act on your consent where required.

Security and international processing

We use technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. We and our providers may process it outside your country. Where required, we use safeguards such as an adequacy decision or approved contractual protections. You can ask us about the safeguards relevant to your information.

Children's privacy

Breezy Pocket Card is intended for a general audience. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has shared personal information without consent required by law, please contact us. We will investigate and delete the information where required.
